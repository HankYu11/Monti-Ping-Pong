*Research date: 12 November 2026. **[Est.]** marks my own estimates; everything else links to a source. Company: 奇斯科技 QI SI TECHNOLOGY, 萬華區, Taipei. Contact address for all outreach: support@handyla.co.*

## 1. In short

- **Sell to coaches, through clubs.** In a Taiwanese 桌球教室 the owner is usually also the head coach, so one person decides. In sports centres and schools the coach is the user, but someone else holds the budget.
- **Start with a free 6-week pilot at 3–5 places run by coaches the founder knows.** Turn them into paying reference clubs before visiting strangers.
- **Price in NT$, low enough to be "one lesson's money"** [Est.]: Coach NT$2,490/year, Club NT$6,900/year, Player NT$990/year, a founding-coach lifetime at NT$2,990 for the first 50 Taiwanese coaches, and a ball kit at NT$990.
- **The main risk is the iPhone.** About 39–50% of new phones sold in Taiwan in 2026 were iPhones ([UAnalyze, Dec 2025](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/4692146931), [yam, May 2026](https://n.yam.com/Article/20260701106899)). The coach needs one Android phone on the tripod; students do not need the app at all. Say this plainly.

## 2. Who decides and what they pay for today

| Type of place | Who decides | Who uses it | What they pay for today | How they'd buy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Private 桌球教室 (one site) | Owner, usually also head coach | Owner and 1–5 hired coaches | Rent, tables, training balls in bulk, ball robots, coach wages. Lessons sell at about NT$1,000/hour one-to-one ([uptogo, Taipei chain price list](https://uptogo.com.tw/?p=1644240)). | Owner pays from the till. Fast decision, price-sensitive. |
| Chain (several sites) | Founder or head office; each site's head coach influences | Many coaches | Same, plus marketing and a brand | Club plan per site, after one site proves it |
| 國民運動中心 | Operator company (e.g. a foundation or contractor) and its course planner | Contract coaches who teach the classes | Courses are sold per term; the centre pays coaches per class [Est.] | Slow. Sell to the coach first; the centre may later buy a station. |
| School team (國小/國中/高中) | Team coach, then the school's PE office for purchases | Coach and team | Balls, robots, travel. Often parent fees or grants ([政大實小 team fee rules](https://www.es.nccu.edu.tw/wp-content/uploads/sites/30/2025/03/政大實小桌球隊收費辦法03.05.pdf)), and city grants for training stations ([Tainan 2026](https://sports.tainan.gov.tw/app_tnsport/views/web/upload_file/MES-1781589449738.pdf)) | Small purchase with a quote and invoice (統一發票). Ask in spring for the next school year. |
| Community club / 協會 / 委員會 | Chair or coach-in-charge | Volunteers and members | Venue hours, events | Low budget. Use them for events and word of mouth, not revenue. |

**What the coach gets out of it** [Est.]: a modern lesson they can sell ("measure your serve, see it improve"), a number students talk about at home and on LINE, and a reason to book serve-specific lessons.

## 3. The offer

| Offer | What's in it | Price (NT$, **all estimates**) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | 30 measured serves a day, full voice result | NT$0 | Students can use this on their own Android phone |
| **Coach** | Unlimited serves, one coach, NEXT SERVE for group turns | **NT$2,490 / year** | €99 is about NT$3,500; NT$2,490 is roughly 2.5 one-to-one lessons in Taipei |
| **Founding coach (Taiwan)** | Coach plan for life, first 50 coaches in Taiwan | **NT$2,990 once** | Matches the €79 global lifetime offer; ends after 50 or 31 March 2027 |
| **Club** | Up to 3 coach phones, club name on the start screen [future], priority help | **NT$6,900 / year** | Same as the €249 club idea, rounded down for Taiwan |
| **Player** | Unlimited serves for one player | **NT$990 / year** | Below €39 because Taiwanese club players are frugal; sold by the club |
| **Ball kit** | 24 marked balls (black half + red/green/blue dots), 12 months Player | **NT$990** | Same as the partner kit floor. Club buys 10 at NT$700 and resells at NT$990 [Est.] |
| **Pilot** | 6 weeks of Coach for free, 24 marked balls on loan, one setup visit | **NT$0** | In return: weekly use, honest feedback, a photo and quote if they like it |

How to take money [Est.]: the first clubs pay QI SI directly by bank transfer against a 統一發票, and get an activation code. This avoids the 15% Play Store fee and lets schools buy with a quote. Move to in-app billing later.

**Sports coin (運動幣):** the 2026 coins end on 31 December 2026 ([經理人](https://www.managertoday.com.tw/articles/view/71651)). A club that is an approved shop could let students use coins for the ball kit. Check the 2027 rules before promising anything.

## 4. The pitch in one sentence

> **「把手機放上腳架，學生每發一球，它就用聲音報出轉速和旋轉種類：讓發球課有看得見的進步。」**
> (Put a phone on a tripod and it speaks the RPM and spin type of every serve, so serve lessons show real progress.)

Supporting lines:
- 世界級發球平均約 2,760 RPM，學生可以直接跟這個數字比。
- 不用網路，球館地下室也能用。
- 一台手機，整組學生輪流發（NEXT SERVE）。

## 5. Demo visit script (30 minutes at the club)

**Bring:** one tested Android phone at 240 fps, a tripod with clamp, 24 marked balls, the marker tool, 5 printed leave-behinds, a sheet to note the coach's phone model.

| Minutes | What happens | What you say or ask |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Greet, ask about the club | 「你們發球課怎麼上？學生最常卡在哪裡？」Listen. Note how many coaches, students, tables. |
| 3–8 | Set up next to one table | Phone at net height, side view. Explain the marked ball in one line: 「黑色半邊加三個色點，手機才看得出轉。」 |
| 8–18 | **The coach serves**, not you | Let the coach do 5 serves: backspin, topspin, side. The phone speaks each result. Ask: 「這個數字跟你的感覺一樣嗎？」 |
| 18–22 | Two students try with NEXT SERVE | Let them compete for the highest RPM. This is usually the moment that sells it. |
| 22–26 | Show the offer | The pilot first: 6 weeks free, balls on loan. Then the Coach and Club prices. |
| 26–30 | Close on a next step | Ask for **one** of: start the pilot this week; a date to come back for a class; the coach's phone model to test. Leave the one-pager. |

After the visit: update the tracker the same day (status, phone model, next step, date).

## 6. Objections and answers

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| **「我用 iPhone。」** | Only the coach's tripod phone needs Android; students just listen. A used Android phone that films 240 fps costs a few thousand NT$ [Est.]; we can suggest tested models, or lend one during the pilot. iOS is on the roadmap but has no date. Don't promise one. |
| **「要自己畫球？太麻煩。」** | With the 3D-printed tool, a ball takes about a minute. Mark 24 once and use them only for serve practice. We supply the first 24 in the pilot; a ready-marked kit is NT$990. |
| **「橘色球可以嗎？」** | Not tested yet. Say so. Use white marked balls for now. |
| **「語音是英文。」** | The words are short and numeric ("2,450 RPM, backspin, strong"). Students learn them in one session. Chinese voice is a request we'll log and count. |
| **「準嗎？」** | It measures spin from 240 fps video of the marks on the ball. Show it live: the coach's own serves are the proof. Accuracy against hand counts is being checked; share the test numbers when published, and don't quote a figure we haven't measured. |
| **「學生資料會存嗎？可以出報告嗎？」** | Not yet. No multi-player profiles or reports in v0.9.2. Ask what report they'd want and write it down: this decides the next feature. |
| **「多少錢？太貴。」** | Start with the free pilot. After that, the Coach plan is about two and a half private lessons a year. |
| **「要網路嗎？」** | No. It works offline. |

## 7. First 3–5 reference clubs: the founder's own coaches

1. **Week 1:** list every coach the founder knows (whether or not they hold a CTTTA licence). For each: where they teach, how many students, phone model (Android?), and whether the place's owner would agree.
2. **Pick 3–5** with: an Android phone or willingness to use a loan phone, at least 2 serve-focused classes a week, and a venue in Taipei or New Taipei (easy to visit). Mix types if possible: one 桌球教室, one school team, one sports-centre class.
3. **Send the "friend" message** (`messages_zh.md`, version D) and book a 30-minute visit.
4. **Run a 6-week pilot.** Visit in week 1 to set up, check in by LINE every week, visit again in week 4.
5. **At week 6 ask for three things:** pay at the founding price, a short quote with a photo, and one introduction to another coach or club owner.
6. These clubs become the names on the leave-behind ("已在這些球館試用") and the first places a stranger can visit to see it working.

## 8. 90-day schedule (from the week the founder starts)

| Weeks | Focus | Activity per week | Which clubs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Prepare and friends | Test 2 loan phones; mark 100 balls; send version D to all known coaches; 2–3 visits | Founder's coaches |
| 3–6 | Pilots running, first cold visits | 1 pilot check-in each; **3 new visits a week** | Taipei private studios and chains first (see `clubs.csv`, "Priority" A), then 萬華/中正/大安 sports centres near the office |
| 7–10 | Convert pilots, widen | Close the pilots; **3–4 visits a week**; 1 talk at a coach gathering if possible | New Taipei (板橋, 中和, 永和, 三重) and Taoyuan; strong school teams |
| 11–13 | Review | 2 visits a week; write up what worked; decide on the south | Taichung/Tainan/Kaohsiung by LINE and video call only, unless a partner (JOOLA Taiwan, 東暎) carries it |

Cold outreach order inside each city [Est.]: private studios and chains (one decider, fast) → school teams (slower, but loyal and visible at tournaments) → sports-centre coaches → associations (for events and endorsement, not revenue).

## 9. Targets and what counts as success (90 days) [Est.]

| Measure | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Clubs contacted | 60 | The full list |
| Demo visits | 25 | About 3 a week from week 3 |
| Pilots started | 8 (3–5 from the founder's network) | |
| **Paying reference clubs** | **3** | The real goal: names a stranger can call |
| Paying coaches | 15 | Founding or Coach plan |
| Revenue | about NT$40,000–60,000 | e.g. 15 coaches at NT$2,490–2,990 plus 2 Club plans and 10 kits |
| Use in pilots | ≥ 2 sessions a week, ≥ 150 serves a week per club | Shows it fits a real lesson |
| Learning | Phone model of every coach visited; top 3 feature requests | Decides iOS and Chinese voice |

**Success:** 3 paying reference clubs and pilot use above the line by day 90.
**Stop or rethink:** fewer than 2 of the first 5 pilots still using it in week 4, or more than 60% of interested coaches unable to use Android.
