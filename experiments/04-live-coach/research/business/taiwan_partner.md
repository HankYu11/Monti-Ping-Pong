# Taiwan: finding a distribution partner

*Research date: 6 November 2026. Facts come from the linked sources. **[Est.]** marks my own reading or estimate. Company context: 奇斯科技 QI SI TECHNOLOGY, a product studio in Wanhua, Taipei, that builds apps, websites and SaaS ([qisi.handyla.co](https://qisi.handyla.co/)).*

The idea: **QI SI provides the app, a partner provides the distribution.**

---

## 0. In short

- **No one in Taiwan sells a spin-measuring app.** Spinsight has no activity in Asia (see `spinsight_deep_dive.md`). What exists in Taiwan:
  - A university lab system that tracks placement and speed, not spin (National Chung Hsing University, NCHU).
  - A sensor racket.
  - App-controlled ball robots.

  ([NCHU](https://gishm.nchu.edu.tw/news_detail.php?Key=164), [科技大觀園](https://scitechvista.nat.gov.tw/Article/c000003/detail?ID=74a03636-bc83-4090-a563-00a8c74f5265), [賓樂達](https://www.synerter.com/news))
- **The best partner fit is an equipment brand or importer that already reaches shops, schools and coaches.**
  - First choice: **JOOLA Taiwan**. JOOLA makes the two-tone Spinball our app reads, and JOOLA has no app for it.
  - Second: **東暎體育**, the Nittaku, Yasaka and DHS agent since 1984.
  - Wild card: **蛋牌 the egg**, a local brand that brought Japan's T.League season opener to Hsinchu in July 2026.
- **Sell a physical kit, not just an app** [Est.]. A box with dotted balls, the 3D-printed marker and an activation code fits how importers sell: through shops, Shopee and schools. It also gives the partner a margin they understand.
- **Coaches are reachable through the national federation's coach licences.** The federation (CTTTA) had about 3,800 licensed coaches at the end of 2025, and a licence must be renewed every 4 years with 48 hours of training ([CTTTA coach plan 2026](https://www.cttta.org.tw/files/News/202622312380.pdf)). A coach course on "measuring spin" is the same route Spinsight used in Germany.
- **Biggest product risk for Taiwan:** the app is Android-only. Check how many Taiwanese coaches and players use iPhones before signing anything that promises reach.

## 3. What the deal could look like [Est.]

| Model | How it works | Partner's take | Our take | When it fits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Kit through the partner** (recommended) | The partner sells a "Spin Training Kit" in shops, on Shopee and to schools: 12–24 dotted two-tone balls, the marker cup and stencil, and a 12-month Player code (or a Coach code in a coach version). | Normal retail margin on the box (30–50%) | A fixed licence fee per activation code, e.g. NT$300–500 | JOOLA, 東暎, the egg |
| B. Revenue share on app subscriptions | The partner promotes the app with its own codes; users pay in the app | 30–40% of what their codes bring in | 60–70%, minus the store's 15% | Coaching chains, influencers |
| C. White label | The partner's brand on the app; they pay a yearly licence plus setup | All the sales | Licence fee (e.g. NT$300k–1M a year) plus custom work | Only once the app is proven |

**Rough kit prices [Est.]:** a retail price of NT$990–1,490 for 12–24 balls, the marker and 12 months of Player. In Taiwan, JOOLA Spinballs and the 3D prints cost us about NT$300–500 a kit. **Check these numbers with the partner before quoting them.** If the partner's shops are approved for 運動幣, part of the price can be paid with the subsidy.

**Terms to propose:**
- 12 months of exclusive Taiwan distribution, with a minimum number of kits (e.g. 500 in year one). If the minimum is missed, the deal becomes non-exclusive.
- We keep the app, the data and every market outside Taiwan.
- A 3-month pilot first: 5 coaching sites, measured use, then the full deal.

The lesson from Spinsight's German applications still applies: ask for a pilot with a public outcome (school PE, grassroots coaching), apply with a partner, and keep the first ask small.

## 5. Next steps

1. **Pick two partners to approach first.** I suggest JOOLA Taiwan and 東暎.
2. **Write a one-page pitch in Chinese** and a 60-second demo video: a JOOLA Spinball serve, the phone speaking the RPM and the spin type.
3. **Run a pilot** with 3–5 coaching sites (a chain, a school team, a sports centre) for 4 weeks. Measure serves per week and what coaches say.
4. **Answer the iPhone question.** Find out what phones Taiwanese coaches use. If most use iPhones, iOS has to come before a national launch.
5. **Apply to the next ASPN cohort** with the partner's letter of intent.
