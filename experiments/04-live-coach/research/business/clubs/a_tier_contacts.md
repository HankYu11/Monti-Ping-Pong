## How this was checked

- Each club's own site (home and linked inner pages) was searched for email and YouTube links.
- YouTube's own search was run for each name; channel pages were opened for size and last upload.
- Facebook pages can't be read from this session (login wall), so two emails come only from search-engine snippets of those pages. Check them on Facebook before sending.
- tain.tw hides emails behind Cloudflare's address protection. I didn't decode them; open the pages in a normal browser.
