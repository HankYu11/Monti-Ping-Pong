How to record

  1. Mark the ball: colour one half black along the seam, draw one 5 mm line over the white half, and add one dot beside it.
  2. Slow motion at 240 fps, phone in landscape on a tripod, level, at contact height.
  3. Side-on, about 1–1.5 m from the ball's path, so the ball is about 50 px across. A plain wall behind the flight; not white, not black.
  4. Bright light: daylight is best. Dim rooms make the marking soft.
  5. Serve across the camera's view. The meter needs about 0.2 s of flight before the first bounce.
  6. Several serves in one video is fine: leave a second or two between serves and keep the camera still. Each serve gets its own reading.
