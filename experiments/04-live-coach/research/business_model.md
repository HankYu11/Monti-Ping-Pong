# Serve Coach Live: Business Model

*Research date: 5 November 2026. Prices change often, so check the linked pages before you rely on them. **[Est.]** marks my own estimates. Everything without that mark comes from the linked source.*

---

## 0. Summary

- **The market has one direct competitor, Spinsight, and it is strong in Germany and France.** It is backed by ESN, the German rubber maker. Since May 2026 it has been the DTTB/TTBL "Official Technical Partner" ([TTBL](https://www.ttbl.de/news/post/6a0ecabf10b7cf0001d52c5e)), and it is the main sponsor of World Table Tennis Day 2026 ([ITTF Foundation](https://ittffoundation.org/news/details/spinsight-announced-as-global-main-sponsor-of-world-table-tennis-day-2026)).
- **Spinsight has clear gaps.** Measuring works only on iPhone 13 or newer, and Android is a closed beta. It needs an internet connection and its own balls, which cost about €2.70 each. Its US App Store listing has only 5 ratings ([spinsight.com](https://spinsight.com/), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/spinsight/id6741476615), [TTD forum](https://www.tabletennisdaily.com/forum/goto/post?id=554721)).
- **Our position is the opposite on each point.** The app runs offline on the phone, speaks each result, gives coaching, and focuses on serves. If experiment E2 passes, it also works with **cheap off-the-shelf two-tone balls** (wholesale about $0.08 per ball).
- **First paying segment: coaches who teach serves** (private coaches and club coaches) in English-speaking markets. They pay for a Coach plan, and their players use a free tier. They are also the sales channel.
- **Suggested prices:** Player €4.99/month or €39/year. Coach €99/year. Optional kit of balls and a clamp at €29.
- **The two biggest unknowns** are (a) whether mid-range Android phones give apps access to 240 fps at all, and (b) whether players will mark balls or buy marked ones. Both can be tested in 4 to 6 weeks for under €1,000 **[Est.]**.
- **This is a niche business unless it grows.** Reaching €40–60k a year in recurring revenue at month 12 is a realistic stretch **[Est.]**. Growing beyond that needs iOS, strokes other than serves, and B2B deals.

### 1.3 Which segment to start with: coaches who teach serves

1. **The workflow fits the product as it is.** The app is turn-based ("serve, wait, Ready") and has a helper NEXT SERVE button. That matches a coach running a serve drill. A player practising alone loses most of the session to picking up marked balls; a coach with a bucket of marked balls does not.
2. **The economics work.** For a coach the tool is a business expense, and one coach reaches 10–40 players. Each of those players is a lead for the Player plan.
3. **It builds trust.** A coach who says "the app agrees with my eye" is the best answer to doubts about accuracy.
4. **It avoids Spinsight's strongest ground.** Spinsight sells to federations, clubs and pros in Germany and France. Private coaches in the UK, US, Australia and Asia, and coaches who use Android, are less served.

Serious juniors and their parents come second, through those coaches. Brands and federations come later, once there is data and credibility.

**Key fact:** marked balls are required by physics (plain balls cannot be read). Spinsight earns money from this need at about €2.70 per ball. Our margin can come from software, and we can make the ball cheap or free (§3.4).

### 3.2 Freemium subscription (recommended as the core)

| | Free | Player | Coach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Live spoken RPM + spin type | **30 serves a day** | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Scale (weak / medium / strong) + world-class markers | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Coaching lines ("you pushed instead of brushing") | First 3 a day | ✓ | ✓ |
| Saved sessions, history, trends, best-serve clips | Last session only | ✓ | ✓ |
| Drive / PDF report export | – | ✓ | ✓ |
| Several player profiles on one phone, per-player reports to share | – | – | ✓ (up to 40) |
| Serve-drill templates (e.g. "10 backspin serves, target above 2,400") | – | ✓ | ✓ + custom |
| AI setup coach (bundled, capped) | Use your own key | 10 sessions/month | 30 sessions/month |
| **Price** | €0 | **€4.99/month or €39/year** | **€12.99/month or €99/year** |

Why these prices:
- Player at €39/year is below Spinsight Player Pro (€60/year) and far below SwingVision Plus/Pro ($96–180/year). For a tool that covers serves only, it should sit lower.
- Coach at €99/year is the price of about **two to three private lessons**. It sits at the low end of OnForm ($199.99/year) and is a third of Spinsight Team Pro (€300/year). A cheaper entry price makes the decision easy for a private coach.

### 3.3 One-time purchase

A "Serve Lab lifetime" for €59–79 (about 1.5–2 times the annual price; Rapsodo's lifetime is about 2.5–3 times annual). This suits buyers who dislike subscriptions and fits the "it all runs on my phone" story. **Use it as a launch offer** (founders' lifetime deal) to fund the iOS port, not as the main model: there is no recurring revenue, and support costs keep running.

### 3.4 Hardware bundle (balls + clamp)

The ball problem is real. Players who mark few balls spend much of the session picking them up.

- **Option A: "Spin Kit" for €29.** It contains 48 two-tone or pre-marked balls, a clamp or tripod adapter that fits a net post or barrier, a printed setup card, and 3 months of Player. Cost **[Est.]**: balls €4–15, clamp €5–8 wholesale, packing and shipping €6–10, totalling about €15–30 delivered. **The margin is thin.** The kit removes friction rather than making profit. Compare Spinsight's Basic kit at €50 (6 balls).
- **Option B: no hardware (preferred if E2 passes).** If the app reliably reads **commodity two-tone balls** (JOOLA Spinball, Sanwei bi-colour), link to them and sell no hardware. "Works with any €0.30 two-tone ball" is a strong message against €2.70 proprietary balls.
- **Option C: marking stencil plus paint marker** for €9 as a fallback.

Keep the bucket-and-pickup problem in the coach pitch: coaches already own ball buckets and nets for multi-ball training.

|---|---|---|

| Club (3 phones, unlimited profiles, club leaderboard) | €249/year | Spinsight Team Pro €300/year (100 players) |
| Academy or federation | €1–5k/year plus custom work **[Est.]** | Partnership deals |

The Club plan needs accounts and sync, which don't exist yet. Postpone it until after month 6.

### 3.6 B2B licensing

- **Rubber brands other than ESN** (Butterfly/Tamca, Nittaku, DHS, Yinhe, Andro, Xiom, Tibhar **[Est. list]**). They could pay for **neutral, published spin tests** ("rubber X serve spin, robot-fed, n=200") and creator campaigns. Forum users already compare rubbers with Spinsight ([TTD](https://www.tabletennisdaily.com/forum/posts/578837/)), which shows the demand. Price **[Est.]**: €2–10k per test campaign.
- **Robot makers** (Pongbot, JOOLA iPong, Amicus). Offer an "RPM calibration" of their spin levels, a co-branded bundle (robot plus app code), or an SDK licence. **[Est.]** €1–3 per bundled licence, or a revenue share.
- **Federations and training centres.** Standardised serve tests for talent scouting. Slow, and Germany and France belong to Spinsight. Possible in England, the US and Asia later.

### 3.7 Data and content

- An anonymised **"serve spin benchmark"** by age and level (e.g. "U15 average backspin serve: X RPM"). This works as marketing, and later as a feature for coaches and federations. It needs opt-in uploads, so it comes after accounts exist.
- Creator content ("spin challenge") is a cheap way to get users, not a revenue stream. Note that TableTennisDaily already ran a spin challenge with a world champion ([TTD](https://www.tabletennisdaily.com/forum/topics/ttd-team-spin-challenge-with-world-champion.38450/)).

## 4. Recommended model

| Element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **First paying segment** | Private and club **coaches** who run serve sessions, in English-speaking markets (UK, US, Australia, Singapore, India) plus your home network. Their players come second. |
| **Offer** | "**Hear your serve's spin. Every serve, in 3 seconds, no internet, no special subscription balls.**" A coach running a drill gets RPM, spin type and a coaching cue spoken aloud, plus a shareable report per student. |
| **Prices** | Coach €99/year (or €12.99/month). Player €39/year (or €4.99/month). Launch offer: founding-coach lifetime at €79 for the first 100 coaches. Optional Spin Kit at €29 if E2 fails. |

## 5. Risks and cheap tests

| Risk | Why it matters | Evidence | Cheap test |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phone compatibility** | Measuring needs 240 fps through Camera2 "constrained high-speed" in a third-party app. Samsung reportedly blocks or limits high-frame-rate capture for third-party apps (developer forums, anecdotal: [Samsung dev forum](https://forum.developer.samsung.com/t/slow-motion-camera-240fps-how-to/12114), [XDA](https://xdaforums.com/t/can-someone-run-camera2probe-and-share-the-results-for-target-fps-range.4592679/)). Spinsight gave up on Android for the same reason. Pixels list "slo-mo up to 240 FPS" ([Google](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/7158570?hl=en)). iPhones from the 8 do 1080p at 240 fps. | Strong warning sign | **E1**: a free "240 fps checker" app or web page that reports the high-speed modes available. If fewer than 30% of interested Android users pass, **move iOS forward**. |
| **Marked-ball friction** | Plain balls can't be read, players hate picking up balls, and self-marking is a chore | Tester feedback; Spinsight gets around it by selling its own balls | **E2**: accuracy on off-the-shelf two-tone balls. **E4**: pre-sale of a kit. |
| **Willingness to pay** | Club players are frugal, and serve-only is narrow | Spinsight shows few public ratings, so its traction outside the DACH region is unclear | **E3**: landing page with prices. **E5**: coach pilot with a paid conversion step. |
| **Trust in accuracy** | "±5% against hand counts" needs proof. One bad reading in front of a student loses a coach. | Only internal tests so far | Publish a validation video and a dataset (50 serves, slow-motion hand count against app). Show a confidence flag and say "unreadable" rather than guessing. |
| **Spinsight's response** | Better funded, with federation channels. It could ship Android or lower prices. | DTTB partnership in 2026, WTT Day sponsor | Stay out of DACH and France at first. Compete on offline use, voice, serve coaching, cheap balls, and older iPhones. Stay small and fast. |
| **Gemini dependency** | Price and model changes; session caps | Live model line-up changes often | Keep the AI coach optional; the rule-based setup guidance already works. |
