Demo visit, 30 minutes

0–3Ask how they teach serves and where students get stuck.
3–8Set up the tripod beside one table, side view, net height.
8–18The coach serves: backspin, topspin, side. 「這個數字跟你的感覺一樣嗎？」
18–22Two students compete with NEXT SERVE.
22–26Offer the pilot, then the prices.
26–30Close on one next step; note the coach's phone model; leave the one-pager.

Objections

「我用 iPhone」Only the coach's tripod phone must be Android; students just listen. Lend a phone in the pilot. iOS has no date; don't promise one.
Marking ballsAbout a minute a ball with the tool. We bring 24; a kit is NT$990. Orange two-tone balls are untested.
English voiceShort words and numbers, learned in one session. Log every request for Chinese.
AccuracyShow it live on the coach's own serves. Don't quote a figure we haven't measured.
ReportsNot yet. Ask what they want; it shapes the next feature.

First 3–5 reference clubs

  1. List every coach the founder knows: venue, students, phone model.
  2. Pick 3–5 with Android (or a loan phone) and 2+ serve classes a week, in Taipei or New Taipei.
  3. Send message D and book a 30-minute visit.
  4. 6-week pilot: set up in week 1, LINE check-in weekly, visit in week 4.
  5. Week 6: ask them to pay at the founding price, give a quote and photo, and introduce one more coach.

Rethink if fewer than 2 of the first 5 pilots still use it in week 4, or if over 60% of interested coaches can't use Android. About 39–50% of new phones sold in Taiwan in 2026 were iPhones (UAnalyze, yam).

Three decisions for the founder

  1. 1. Prices. Approve the NT$ list (Coach 2,490/yr, founding lifetime 2,990 for 50 coaches, Club 6,900/yr, kit 990), or set your own before the first visit.
  2. 2. Android gap. Buy 2–3 used 240 fps Android phones to lend during pilots (about NT$10–20k in total, estimate), or only pilot with coaches who already own one.
  3. 3. Who goes first. Name the 3–5 coaches you know for the pilot and pick which one week to start; cold visits wait until at least 2 are running.
